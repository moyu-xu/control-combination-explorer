# Third-party notices

The application bundles open-source Python and JavaScript dependencies. Before
distributing a release, generate and review the exact dependency inventory from
`uv.lock` and `frontend/package-lock.json`, and include the corresponding
license texts in the installer.

Core components include FastAPI, Uvicorn, React, Vite, NumPy, pandas, SciPy,
PyReadStat, PyFixest, OpenPyXL, python-docx, PyInstaller, and their transitive
dependencies. Their respective copyrights and licenses remain with their
authors.
